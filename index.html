Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy NFC tags in bulk in Canada?

We sell NTAG213, NTAG215, NTAG216 and NTAG 424 DNA tags, NFC cards and NFC wristbands in bulk from 100 units, priced in Canadian dollars. Orders ship from the GTA, so Canadian customers pay no duty or brokerage fees, and we ship worldwide.

Which NFC chip should I choose?

For a single link, such as a menu, review page or website, NTAG213 works and is the cheapest. Choose NTAG215 or NTAG216 for longer data like contact cards. Choose NTAG 424 DNA only if the tag must be impossible to copy, such as for product authentication.

What's the difference between NTAG213, NTAG215 and NTAG216?

Memory. NTAG213 holds 144 bytes, NTAG215 holds 504 bytes and NTAG216 holds 888 bytes. They all read the same way on any NFC phone.

Do NFC tags work with iPhones?

Yes. iPhone XS and newer read NFC tags in the background without an app, as do almost all modern Android phones.

Do NFC tags need a battery?

No. The phone powers the tag through its NFC field when it is held close, usually within 1 to 4 cm.

How long do NFC tags last?

NXP NTAG chips keep their data for at least 10 years and can be rewritten about 100,000 times.

Can you program the tags for us?

Yes. We can write a URL, vCard, Wi-Fi login or app link to every tag, give each tag a unique link, and lock them before shipping.

What's the minimum order?

100 units for stock tags. Custom-printed and custom-format orders usually start at 250 to 500 units, depending on the product.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. Businesses can request a sample pack of our standard tags. Mention it in your message.

Do you ship outside Canada?

Yes, we ship worldwide with tracking. Shipping is quoted with your order.

Do you charge customs or duty?

Not on Canadian orders, since they ship from within Canada. International orders may be charged import duties or taxes by the destination country. Prices are in CAD, and HST applies to Canadian orders.
