About NFC wristbands

Silicone NFC wristbands with an NTAG213 chip sealed inside. They are waterproof, comfortable for all-day wear and reusable.

Event organizers use them for entry and cashless payment, gyms for check-in, and trade show teams for lead capture. Custom colours and printed logos are available on larger orders.

Common uses

  • Festival and event entry
  • Cashless payment at events
  • Gym and club check-in
  • Trade show lead capture
  • Resorts and water parks
