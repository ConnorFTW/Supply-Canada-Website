About NFC cards

These are white PVC cards in the standard CR80 size (the same as a credit card) with an NTAG213 chip inside. They work with any NFC phone and print on standard ID card printers.

They are popular as digital business cards, tap-to-review cards and loyalty or membership cards. We can encode each card with the same link or a unique one, and print your design on both sides.

Common uses

  • Digital business cards
  • Tap-to-review cards for counters
  • Loyalty and membership cards
  • Check-in cards for gyms and clubs

Questions about NFC cards

Can you print our design on the cards?

Yes. Send us your artwork and quantity and we will quote full-colour printing.

Will these cards work with our door access system?

Only if your system reads NFC Forum Type 2 tags. Many access systems use MIFARE or HID cards instead, so check with your installer first.
