About NTAG213 NFC stickers

NTAG213 is the most widely used NFC chip for stickers. It holds 144 bytes, which is enough for one web address of about 130 characters, and every NFC-enabled iPhone and Android phone can read it without an app.

Because it is the lowest-cost NXP NTAG chip, it is the right pick when every tag just needs to open a link. If you need to store a contact card or several records, look at NTAG215 or NTAG216 instead.

Common uses

  • Google review and social media stickers on tables and counters
  • Digital menus in restaurants and cafés
  • Product information on packaging
  • Business card and phone stickers
  • Shortcuts for smart home routines

Questions about NTAG213 NFC stickers

How long a URL fits on an NTAG213 sticker?

About 130 characters. The https:// and www. parts are stored in one byte, so most links fit easily.

Can NTAG213 stickers be rewritten?

Yes. Each tag can be rewritten about 100,000 times, or locked permanently so nobody can change it.

Do NTAG213 stickers work on metal?

Standard stickers do not read reliably when stuck directly on metal. Ask us for an on-metal version, which has a shielding layer.
